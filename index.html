Office Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Office Rush (the game; Android package com.adaeternum.officerush) is a single-player mobile game published on Google Play and RuStore, and a Windows version distributed through VK Play. It is developed by adaeternum. This page explains what data the game handles.

Short version: the game collects nothing, sends nothing and works entirely offline.

1. Data we collect

None. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs and no network access at all. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally, in its own private app storage:

  • progress — levels completed, stars, best times, found secret items;
  • settings — sound, music, vibration, difficulty, language, fullscreen and graphics options.

This data never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings (Apps → Office Rush → Storage → Clear data).

3. Permissions

The only permission the game requests is VIBRATE — short tactile feedback on taking damage, picking items up or winning. Vibration can be turned off in the game settings.

4. Third parties

We share no data with third parties because we receive none. The stores that distribute the game (Google Play, RuStore, VK Play) may collect their own install and diagnostic data under their own privacy policies; that processing is outside the game and outside our control.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date above changed. The current version is always available at this address.
