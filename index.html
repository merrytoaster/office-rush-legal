Office Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Office Rush (the game; Android package com.adaeternum.officerush) is a single-player mobile game published on Google Play and RuStore, and a Windows version distributed through VK Play. It is developed by adaeternum. This page explains what data the game handles.

Short version: the developer collects nothing. Your progress stays on your device. Ads between levels are served by Yandex Mobile Ads, which uses a device advertising identifier — and you can turn ads off for good with a one-time purchase.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect personal data ourselves: there are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no servers of ours. The only data that leaves the device comes from the advertising SDK described in section 4.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps its data locally, in its own private app storage:

  • progress — levels completed, stars, best times, found secret items;
  • settings — sound, music, vibration, difficulty, language, fullscreen and graphics options;
  • the flag that ads were turned off by a purchase.

This data never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings (Apps → Office Rush → Storage → Clear data).

3. Permissions

  • VIBRATE — short tactile feedback on taking damage, picking items up or winning; can be turned off in the game settings;
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — needed only to request ads. Without network access the game is fully playable, it just cannot fetch an ad.

4. Advertising (Yandex Mobile Ads)

Between levels the game shows a full-screen ad served by Yandex Mobile Ads. To select, deliver and measure ads the SDK may process: the device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), device model, OS version, language, IP address, and events such as ad impressions and clicks. This data is used to serve ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, prevent fraud and measure performance, and may be transferred to Yandex as the ad provider. Its processing is governed by Yandex's own privacy policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential.

We do not receive this data. We only see aggregate revenue statistics that contain no personal data.

5. Purchases

The game offers a one-time, non-consumable purchase that turns ads off. Payments are processed by Google Play: we never see your name, card or payment details. The only thing the game receives from Google Play is the fact that the purchase exists, and it is stored on the device as the "ads disabled" flag. The purchase is restored automatically when you reinstall the game or move to another device signed in to the same Google account.

6. Third parties

Two categories of third parties are involved: the store that distributes the game (Google Play, RuStore, VK Play — its own install and diagnostic data under its own policy) and Yandex Mobile Ads (section 4). We share nothing else with anyone, because we hold no data.

7. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads) — this limits personalised advertising;
  • turn ads off permanently with the in-game purchase (Settings → Remove ads);
  • uninstall the game — all local data is deleted with it.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and ads in it are not directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date above changed. The current version is always available at this address.

10. Contact
